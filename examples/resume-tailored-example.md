# Resume Tailoring Example

> Sanitized example. All names, companies, numbers, and identifying details replaced with placeholders.

---

## Target Role
[Role Title] at [Company] — selling [product category] into [buyer type]

---

## Summary

**Before:**
Results-driven sales professional with experience in enterprise software and a passion for building client relationships.

**After:**
[N]+ years selling [product category] into [buyer type] — [$X] in closed revenue, [X]% average quota attainment. Background spans [Company A] and [Company B]; consistent top-X% performance in both. Deep familiarity with [buyer pain] and the [specific workflow] that [target company's product] addresses.

---

## Experience Bullets

**Before:**
- Responsible for managing key accounts and growing revenue in assigned territory

**After:**
- Grew territory revenue from $X to $X over [N] years, maintaining X% quota attainment while expanding average contract value

---

**Before:**
- Helped grow the sales team

**After:**
- Recruited and onboarded 3 account executives; all exceeded quota in year one

---

## Gaps to Address in Interview

- JD requires experience with [specific methodology] — not explicitly on resume. Prepare to connect [adjacent experience] to this requirement.
- JD mentions [industry vertical] — resume is primarily [different vertical]. Prepare the bridge: what transfers and what you'd learn.

---

## What Made the Difference

The most important tailoring move here was replacing the filler summary with a version that:
1. Mirrors the exact language of the JD
2. Leads with a number
3. Names the specific companies (signals transparency)
4. Connects directly to the buyer pain the target product solves
