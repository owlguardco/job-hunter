# LinkedIn Audit Example

> Sanitized example. All names, companies, and identifying details replaced with placeholders.

---

## Headline

**Before:** Senior Account Executive at [Company A]

**After:** Helping [Buyer Type] Teams Stay [Outcome] | [Product Category]

**Rating:** Critical Fix

**Feedback:** The original is a job title. Job titles tell recruiters where you've been, not what you do for a buyer. A buyer who lands on this profile from a search sees nothing that tells them why they should connect. The rewritten version leads with buyer value and contains keywords a recruiter hiring for this role would search.

---

## About Section

**Before:**
> I am a results-driven sales professional with a proven track record in enterprise software. I am passionate about building relationships and helping organizations solve complex challenges. I bring strong communication skills and a collaborative approach to every engagement.

**After:**
> Seven years selling [product category] into [buyer type] — $X in closed revenue, [notable achievement]. The work I find most interesting is the consultative piece: understanding how [buyer pain] actually shows up inside an organization and building a case that connects to what a CFO cares about.
>
> Background: [Company A] (20XX-20XX), [Company B] (20XX-20XX). Territory covered [geography]. Quota attainment averaged X% over five years.

**Rating:** Critical Fix

**Feedback:** The original contains four filler phrases that appear in thousands of profiles. None of them add signal. The rewrite leads with numbers, is written in first person as a human, and tells a coherent story about what this person actually does.
