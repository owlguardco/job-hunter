# Cover Letter Example

> Sanitized example. All names, companies, and identifying details replaced with placeholders.

---

## Target Role
[Role Title] at [Company] — selling [product] into [buyer type]

---

## Output

[Role Title] — [Company]

[N] years selling [product category] into [buyer type] taught me one thing about the [specific pain] problem: the organizations that solve it fastest are the ones that treat it as a revenue issue, not a compliance checkbox. That's the motion I understand, and it's exactly what [Company]'s product enables.

At [Previous Company], I sold [adjacent product] into [similar buyer type] — [specific relevant achievement with number]. The buyers were [buyer titles], the conversations were about [specific pain], and the business case always came back to [quantifiable outcome]. The overlap with what [Company] is doing in [market] is direct.

What draws me to this specific role is [specific detail from JD or company that is real, not generic]. [One sentence connecting that detail to something true in your background.]

I'd welcome the chance to talk through how this maps to what you're building.

[Name]

---

## What Makes This Work

- Opens with a substantive observation about the market, not an announcement of intent
- The first paragraph establishes the angle — [specific pain] as a revenue issue — which mirrors the JD's framing
- Numbers appear where relevant, not forced
- Close is direct and confident, not a plea
- Total word count: under 300
- No em dashes, no filler phrases, no "Hi"
