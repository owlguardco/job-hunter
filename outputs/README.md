# Outputs

This is where Job Hunter saves your results.

**This folder is git-ignored.** Nothing here ever gets pushed to GitHub.
Your resume, job descriptions, and generated content stay on your machine only.

## Using your outputs

Copy the content into Word, Google Docs, or your email client.
Don't submit markdown files directly — format them first.

The cover letter and resume outputs are especially important to read out loud
before using. If any sentence sounds like a bot wrote it, rewrite it.
