# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Job searching is already stressful. This project exists to reduce that. Be direct, be useful, and don't make it harder for anyone who shows up here.

## Expected Behavior

- Give specific, actionable feedback — vague criticism helps no one
- Be honest about what works and what doesn't, including your own contributions
- Respect that people are in different stages of their career and search
- Credit people's work when you build on it

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Sharing another person's resume, job application, or personal information without their consent
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or solicitation
- Dismissing contributions because someone is early in their career

## Scope

This applies to the GitHub repo, issues, pull requests, and any project-affiliated spaces.

## Enforcement

If someone is violating this code of conduct, open a GitHub issue or contact the maintainers directly. Violations will result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban depending on severity and history.

## The Standard

If you wouldn't say it to someone who is three weeks into a job search and running out of savings, don't say it here.
