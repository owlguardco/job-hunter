# Legal Documents

Required for the hosted commercial version at jobhunter.ai.

| Document | Required by | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) | Stripe, Clerk, most jurisdictions | ✓ Done |
| [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) | Best practice, liability protection | ✓ Done |
| [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md) | Stripe requirement | ✓ Done |

## Before launch checklist

- [ ] Update "Last updated" dates when you make material changes
- [ ] Add your actual contact email to all three documents
- [ ] Link all three from the footer of `commercial/web/index.html`
- [ ] Ensure Stripe Checkout shows the refund policy URL
- [ ] Add Privacy Policy link to Clerk sign-up flow (Clerk dashboard → customization)

## What's NOT included (intentionally)

- **Cookie policy** — not needed (no ad or tracking cookies)
- **GDPR/CCPA compliance page** — not needed until you actively market to EU/CA users
- **Accessibility statement** — not needed at launch
- **DPA (Data Processing Agreement)** — not needed until enterprise customers ask

## Jurisdiction note

These documents are written for Tennessee, US law. If you incorporate elsewhere
or market heavily to EU users, consult a lawyer before launch.

These documents were drafted for general use and are not legal advice.
Consider having a lawyer review them before taking your first payment.
