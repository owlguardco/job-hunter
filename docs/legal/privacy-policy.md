# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 21, 2026
**Effective date:** May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Job Hunter ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by
owlguardco, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our hosted
service at jobhunter.ai.

---

## What we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account information** — your email address, collected when you sign up via Clerk
- **Resume and job content** — the text you paste into our tools (resume, job descriptions,
  LinkedIn profiles, interview notes). This content is used only to generate tool output
  and is not shared with any third party
- **Profile data** — if you choose to save your resume or target role to your account,
  this is stored in our database and used only to pre-fill tool inputs for your convenience

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage logs** — which tools you run, when, and how many credits were consumed.
  We use this to track billing and improve the service. We do not log the content
  of your inputs or outputs.
- **Authentication data** — Clerk manages your login session. We receive only your
  user ID and email address from Clerk.
- **Payment data** — Stripe processes all payments. We receive only purchase confirmation
  and your Stripe customer ID. We never see your card number, expiry, or CVV.

---

## How we use your information

- To provide the service — running tool prompts using your input content
- To manage your account and credit balance
- To process payments via Stripe
- To respond to support requests
- To send transactional emails (purchase confirmations, account notices) via Clerk

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not sell your data.
We do not use your resume or job content to train AI models.

---

## Who we share data with

| Service | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthropic** | Your input content (resume, JD, etc.) when you run a tool | To generate tool output |
| **Clerk** | Your email address | To manage authentication |
| **Stripe** | Your email address + purchase details | To process payments |
| **Railway** | Server logs (no content) | To host the service |

No other third parties receive your data.

**Anthropic's data handling:** Your content is sent to Anthropic's API to generate
output. Anthropic's privacy policy and data retention terms apply to this content.
See [anthropic.com/privacy](https://anthropic.com/privacy). As of this writing,
Anthropic does not use API inputs to train models by default.

---

## Data retention

- **Usage logs** — retained for 12 months, then deleted
- **Saved profile** — retained until you delete it or close your account
- **Session data** — deleted after 2 hours of inactivity
- **Purchase records** — retained for 7 years (tax and legal requirement)
- **Account data** — retained until you close your account, then deleted within 30 days

---

## Your rights

You can:
- **Access your data** — email us and we will provide a copy of what we store about you
- **Delete your account** — we will delete your profile, usage history, and session data
  within 30 days. Purchase records are retained for legal compliance.
- **Delete your saved profile** — use the profile settings in your dashboard, or email us
- **Opt out of analytics** — we don't run behavioral analytics, so there's nothing to opt out of

---

## Cookies

We use only session cookies necessary for authentication (set by Clerk).
We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies.

---

## Security

Your data is stored on Railway-hosted infrastructure in the United States.
We use HTTPS for all connections. Passwords are managed by Clerk and never stored by us.
Credit card data is processed by Stripe and never stored by us.

---

## Children

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13.
