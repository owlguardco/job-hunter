# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** May 21, 2026

---

## Credit packs (one-time purchases)

Credit packs are **non-refundable** once purchased, with one exception:

**If you purchase credits and experience a technical failure on our end that
prevents you from using them**, we will issue a full refund or credit
replacement at our discretion.

Technical failures covered:
- Server outage preventing tool runs
- Billing error (charged incorrectly)
- Credits not added after confirmed payment

Technical failures not covered:
- You were unsatisfied with the AI output
- You changed your mind after purchase
- You found a job and no longer need the credits

To request a refund for a qualifying technical failure, open an issue at
[github.com/owlguardco/job-hunter](https://github.com/owlguardco/job-hunter)
with your purchase date and a description of the issue. We respond within
48 hours.

---

## Monthly subscriptions

You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time.

- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- You retain full access until the period ends
- We do not issue partial refunds for unused days in a billing period
- If you cancel within 24 hours of your first monthly charge and have run
  fewer than 5 tools, contact us and we will issue a full refund

To cancel: go to your dashboard → account settings → cancel subscription.

---

## How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe and typically
appear within 5-10 business days depending on your bank.
