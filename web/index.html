ATS Scanner
Find out why you're not getting callbacks — even when you're qualified for the role.
💡 What is ATS and why does it matter?
Most companies use software called an ATS — Applicant Tracking System — to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. When you apply online and never hear back, it's usually not because a recruiter looked at your resume and passed. It's because software scanned it first and filtered you out automatically.

Here's how it works: the ATS scans your resume for keywords from the job description, checks the formatting for things it can read cleanly, and scores you against other applicants. If your score is too low, your resume goes into a folder no one opens.

Common reasons people get filtered out: missing keywords that appear in the job posting, two-column layouts the software can't parse, special bullet characters that show up as garbage text, no summary section, or a job title that doesn't match what the role is called.

This tool scans your resume the way an ATS does, gives you a match score, and tells you exactly what to fix — in priority order — before you submit another application.
