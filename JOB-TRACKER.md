## Rules

- One application per company — no spraying multiple roles at the same employer
- Never tell Company A about Company B until you have an offer in hand
- Follow up once after applying, once after each interview — then wait
- A competing offer is your only real leverage on comp — never let either company know until offer is signed or imminent
- Do not start emails with "Hi" — reads as AI-generated
